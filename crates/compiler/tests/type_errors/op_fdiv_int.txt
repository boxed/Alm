-- TYPE MISMATCH -------------------------------------------------- src/Main.elm

The (/) operator is specifically for floating-point division:

11|     n / 2
        ^
The left side of (/) must be a Float, but I am seeing an Int. I recommend:

toFloat for explicit conversions     (toFloat 5 / 2) == 2.5
(//)    for integer division         (5 // 2)        == 2

Note: Read <https://elm-lang.org/0.19.1/implicit-casts> to learn why Elm does
not implicitly convert Ints to Floats.
